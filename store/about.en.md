Models Viewer is a small museum of open 3D models that fits on your handheld. It brings together 61 models from the open community, all CC0 or CC BY 4.0, prepared to run smoothly on the R36S.

## What's inside

- **Characters**: KayKit's knight, mage, barbarian and rogue, and two skeletons, each with idle, walk, run, attack and other animations.
- **Animals and toys**: the Khronos fox, Google's scanned dinosaurs, a shark, plush toys and a wooden sheep.
- **Home, gadgets, food and art**: Poly Haven's photo-scanned furniture, cameras, clocks, fruit and sculpture, Khronos showcase models and NVIDIA's SimReady V8 engine, robotic arm and tools.

## On the stage

Each model turns on a softly lit stage with a key, fill and rim light, a studio reflection and a shadow on the floor. Switch styles with Y: studio, clay, wireframe or normals. Animated models play their clips, and Select moves to the next one. L2 and R2 zoom, and L and R step to the neighbouring model.

Press X for the information panel: the authors, license and source of the model, its size, triangles, vertices, materials, textures, joints and animations, and what was changed for the handheld.
